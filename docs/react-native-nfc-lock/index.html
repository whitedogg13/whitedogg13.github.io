GETTING STARTED

From access card to phone

Have your access card and a compatible REAL NFC lock ready.

  1. Download NFC SmartLock

    Install the app from the App Store on iPhone or Google Play on Android.

  2. Import your access card

    Open the app and follow the instructions to read your card.

  3. Hold your phone near the lock

    Start a scan in the app and follow the on-screen instructions to lock or unlock.

Frequently asked questions

Why did the link stay in my browser?

Browser and device settings affect how links open. Open NFC SmartLock from your phone’s home screen, or try the original link again from a message or email.

What happens when the link opens the app?

If you’ve already imported your access card, the link can start an NFC scan in the app. Keep your phone close to the physical lock and follow the on-screen instructions.
